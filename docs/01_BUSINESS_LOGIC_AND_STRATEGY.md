# 01. Business Logic & Global Strategy

## 1. Brand Vision & Operating Philosophy
This fashion brand operates on a **decentralized, dual-hub sourcing and fulfillment model**. By anchoring operations in **Bangladesh** and **India**, the brand leverages two of the world's most distinct and complementary textile ecosystems:
* **Bangladesh**: Renowned for world-class garment manufacturing, sustainable denim, fine knitwear, and heritage handloom (Muslin and Jamdani).
* **India**: Renowned for luxury artisanal handlooms, silk sarees (Kanjeevaram, Banarasi, Chanderi), hand-block printing, and intricate embroidery.

---

## 2. The Symmetric Dual-Hub Model

Rather than running a traditional centralized warehouse with expensive international redistribution, each hub operates **symmetrically as both a domestic fulfillment center and an international export hub**.

## 4. Cross-Border "Global" Policy Between Hubs

A core business rule governing cross-border sales between the two host countries:

> **Rule:** If an **Indian customer** purchases an item physically stocked only in the **Bangladesh Hub**, they are treated as an **international (Global) customer** for that item.
>
> Likewise, if a **Bangladesh customer** purchases an item physically stocked only in the **India Hub**, they are treated as an **international (Global) customer** for that item.

### Why This Rule Exists:
1. **Customs & Regulatory Integrity**: Cross-border garment movements between India and Bangladesh require formal customs clearance, commercial export invoices, and HS code declarations. Treating them as international items ensures legal compliance.
2. **Transparent Customer Expectation**: The customer clearly understands they are ordering an imported piece with international delivery times (5–8 days) and cross-border shipping fees.
3. **Zero Deadstock Shuffling**: Inventory does not need to be pre-shipped or held on consignment across borders before a sale occurs.
